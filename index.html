About me

I grew up in Hungary, spent the last eight years working as a translator in Belfast, and have just made Poznań my home. Living between three countries taught me that translation isn't about words — it's about what people actually mean.

In the UK I worked with agencies, law firms and small businesses on everything from certified documents to marketing campaigns. Now in Poland, I'm helping local companies reach Hungarian and English-speaking markets — and vice versa.

Music has been the other half of my life since I was six. I've taught piano and guitar for over a decade — patiently, in three languages, to kids and adults alike.
